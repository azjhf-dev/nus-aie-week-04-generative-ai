# Generative AI and its Applications

## Published scope

Official module link: https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/executive-education/course/sctp-generative-ai-and-its-applications/

Published classroom days: 2. The week below is an original expanded self-study schedule, not the official timetable.

## Learning outcomes
- Study reconstruction and a nonlinear autoencoder
- Explore forward noise and denoising assumptions
- Compare masked-token and autoregressive objectives and sampling temperature

## Five-day learning plan
- Day 1: reconstruction and autoencoding (3h)
- Day 2: latent controls and denoising (3h)
- Day 3: token probabilities and text objectives (3h)
- Day 4: multimodal alignment and responsible use (3h)
- Day 5: gallery, evaluation and limitations (4h)

## Project
A transparent generative-model gallery with failure notes

## Boundaries
Toy denoising is not Stable Diffusion. Sampling from a small fixed vocabulary is not GPT; no pretrained BERT/GPT or multimodal model inference is bundled. The official named model families need the documented optional extensions.

## Completion evidence
- Clean notebook execution and changed-control screenshots
- Exercise code and deterministic tests
- Lab log with at least two failures
- Dataset/model card where appropriate
- Assessment answers before reading the solutions
